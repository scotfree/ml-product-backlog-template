# Epic: Monitoring & Maintenance

## What this epic covers

How you'll know the system is still working next month. What happens when data shifts or something breaks. Often skipped in student projects; often the first thing real jobs ask about.

## Cards in this epic

1. Failure-mode inventory
2. Logging and metrics in production
3. Retraining and update process

## Success criteria

_How will you know this epic is done? What's the demoable outcome — e.g. a synthetic failure is triggered and the logs and metrics surface it without anyone reading the code?_
