# Survey candidates and pick a baseline

## Description

_What's out there? What can you defensibly start from?_

## Upstream / Downstream contract

- _Whoever handles deployment needs to know model size and dependencies._
- _Whoever handles analysis needs to know what alternatives you considered._

## Definition of Done

_Short doc: 3–5 candidates with sizes and licenses, one paragraph justifying the baseline pick._

## Demo

_Explain the top two candidates and why the winner won._

## Subtasks

- _Include at least one small/fast option and one larger/stronger option._
- _Confirm the baseline runs on the team's hardware before committing._
- _Flag any deployment or license constraints._

## Estimate

_Rough size — minutes, hours, or days. If it's more than a few days, break it up._
