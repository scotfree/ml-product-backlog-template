# Survey candidates and pick a baseline

_What's out there? What can you defensibly start from?_

- **Upstream/downstream:** _Whoever handles deployment needs to know model size and dependencies. Whoever handles analysis needs to know what alternatives you considered._
- **Definition of done:** _Short doc: 3–5 candidates with sizes and licenses, one paragraph justifying the baseline pick._
- **Demo:** _Explain the top two candidates and why the winner won._
- **Subtasks:**
  - _Include at least one small/fast option and one larger/stronger option._
  - _Confirm the baseline runs on the team's hardware before committing._
  - _Flag any deployment or license constraints._
