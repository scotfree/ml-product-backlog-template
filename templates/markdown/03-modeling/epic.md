# Epic: Modeling

## What this epic covers

Choosing what to train and actually training it. In practice these aren't separable — you don't pick an architecture without running trials, and you don't train without picking. One epic.

## Cards in this epic

1. Survey candidates and pick a baseline
2. End-to-end training run, even if bad
3. Add experiment tracking
4. Iterate — improve one thing per run

## Success criteria

_How will you know this epic is done? What's the demoable outcome — e.g. a tracked series of runs ending in a best-so-far checkpoint that beats the baseline?_
