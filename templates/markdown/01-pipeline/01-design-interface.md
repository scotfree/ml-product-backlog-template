# Design the pipeline interface

_Sketch the stages, their inputs, and their outputs. Decide before you code._

- **Upstream/downstream:** _Who consumes your final output? What format do they expect?_
- **Definition of done:** _A one-page diagram or doc that a teammate can read and know what each stage sends and receives._
- **Demo:** _Walk the team through the diagram in 2 minutes; take questions._
- **Subtasks:**
  - _List the stages._
  - _For each stage, name its input and output types._
  - _Identify where stages need to be swappable (mock vs. real)._
