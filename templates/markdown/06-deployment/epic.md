# Epic: Deployment

## What this epic covers

Making the model usable by something other than the training script. Someone else runs it; it does the thing.

## Cards in this epic

1. Package the model for use
2. Runnable by someone who isn't you
3. Benchmark in the target environment

## Success criteria

_How will you know this epic is done? What's the demoable outcome — e.g. a teammate installs the artifact on target hardware and gets a correct result within the latency budget?_
