# Portfolio-quality writeup

_The artifact you'd show in an interview. Short, honest, specific._

- **Upstream/downstream:** _You. Your interviewers._
- **Definition of done:** _2–4 pages (or equivalent slides): what you built, key decisions, what worked, what you'd do next._
- **Demo:** _Present it to the team in 5 minutes. Get feedback._
- **Subtasks:**
  - _Include real numbers, not marketing._
  - _Include one thing that didn't work and what you learned._
