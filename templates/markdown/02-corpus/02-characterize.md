# Characterize the chosen dataset

_Distributions, imbalance, weirdness, duplicates, obvious errors. What will surprise you later?_

- **Upstream/downstream:** _Whoever trains models needs to know what to expect. Whoever evaluates needs to know what edge cases exist._
- **Definition of done:** _A notebook or short report with the key stats and 3–5 things worth flagging._
- **Demo:** _Show 2–3 charts and the one thing that surprised you most._
- **Subtasks:**
  - _Class/label distribution._
  - _Sample size, resolution, or other basic shape stats._
  - _Spot-check: look at ~50 raw examples with your own eyes._
