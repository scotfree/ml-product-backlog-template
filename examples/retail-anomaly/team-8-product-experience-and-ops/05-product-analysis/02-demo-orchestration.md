# Demo orchestration

Week 9 demo runs a seeded scenario end-to-end through the system. T8 owns the flow, transitions, and fallback.

### Upstream / Downstream contract

Team-internal.

### Cross-team contact points

- **T1 Data** provides the seeded demo scenario via replay tool.
- **T7 Platform** provides operating environment; coordinates on live vs recorded fallback.
- **Every producing team** must have their service running and stable during the demo.
- **T6 Explain** provides the "why this matters" narrative for the manager view.
- Timing: Week 8 rehearsal; Week 9 demo.

### Definition of Done

- Written demo script with per-team cues
- Rehearsed at least twice with all teams
- Recorded backup exists

### Demo
