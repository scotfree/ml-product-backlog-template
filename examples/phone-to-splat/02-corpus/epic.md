# Epic: Corpus (Phone-to-Splat)

**Students build the corpus.** No public dataset here — the team designs a capture protocol, chooses reference objects with known dimensions, captures the videos themselves, and — critically — designs an accuracy protocol to compare reconstructions against ground truth.

This is arguably the richest epic in this project. The third card ("Define the accuracy protocol") forces students to invent a measurement methodology, which is a higher-order engineering skill most curricula never teach.

## Cards in this epic

1. Design the capture protocol and reference-object menu
2. Capture, measure, and share the corpus
3. Define the accuracy protocol
