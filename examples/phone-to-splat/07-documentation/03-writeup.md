# Portfolio-quality writeup

Each student produces a 2–4 page writeup for interviews. What they built, what they decided, what worked, what didn't, what they'd do differently.

- **Upstream/downstream:** The student's future interviewers.
- **Definition of done:** Each student has `writeups/<name>.md` (or a linked blog post). Includes real benchmarks, one honest "didn't work" story, and a clear statement of what they personally owned.
- **Demo:** Present to the team in 5 minutes each in the last week; team gives feedback.
- **Subtasks:**
  - Real numbers (accuracy per scene tier, runtime per hardware target) — not marketing.
  - One thing that didn't work and what was learned.
  - Include at least two "why" decisions from the decisions log.
  - Suggested angle for this project: emphasize the *measurement design* work (accuracy protocol) — that skill is rare and interview-visible in a way that most 3D reconstruction projects don't showcase.
