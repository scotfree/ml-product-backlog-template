# Portfolio-quality writeup

Each student produces a 2–4 page writeup they can point at in interviews. What they built, what they decided, what worked, what didn't, what they'd do differently. Honest and specific — real numbers, not marketing.

- **Upstream/downstream:** The student's future interviewers.
- **Definition of done:** Each student has a writeup in `writeups/<name>.md` (or a linked personal blog post). Includes real benchmarks, one honest "didn't work" story, and a clear statement of what they personally owned.
- **Demo:** Present to the team in 5 minutes each in the last week; team gives feedback.
- **Subtasks:**
  - Include real numbers (mAP, latency, wheel size) — not marketing.
  - Include one thing that didn't work and what you learned.
  - Include the "why" for at least two decisions (pulled from the decisions log).
  - Make personal contribution clear — this is a portfolio piece, not a team report.
